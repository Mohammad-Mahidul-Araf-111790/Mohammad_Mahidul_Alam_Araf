01

Hybrid Quantum MCMC and Spiking Neural Networks

Undergraduate thesis, CSE400 — research under way

A formal framework treating quantum Markov chain Monte Carlo as a probabilistic routing mechanism for spiking neural networks, with spectral-gap and error characterisation. The sampler is validated against a 2D Gaussian, a Gaussian mixture, Neal's Funnel and a 100-dimensional correlated Gaussian.

Effective sample size
> 100
Gelman–Rubin R̂
< 1.1
Noise reduction, ZNE
~88%
Code and tests
~1,700 LOC

PennyLane · snnTorch · Mitiq · PyTorch
